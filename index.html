App Support & FAQ

Welcome to BetaNatura Support. If you have any questions about your 30-day capsule ritual, hydration tracking, or account management, find quick answers below or reach out to our team directly.

Q: How does Apple Health / Health Connect integration work?

BetaNatura optionally reads your daily step count and morning weight from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android) to display on your Today and Progress screens. You can enable or disable this anytime under Account → Integrations.

Q: How do I customize my daily capsule and water reminders?

Open the Account tab, enable Reminders, and tap on Capsules, Evening Catch-up, or Water Check times to choose the exact hour that fits your routine.

Q: What is the difference between the Standard and Ease-In plans?

The Standard plan schedules 3 capsules daily from Day 1. The Ease-In plan starts with 1 capsule daily for the first 3 days and transitions to 3 capsules daily from Day 4 onward.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

BetaNatura ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal and wellness data. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your first name, email address, and encrypted authentication credentials via Firebase Authentication.
  • Daily Ritual & Progress Logs: Capsule intake counts, daily water intake, and weight logs that you enter to track your 30-day progress.
  • Health Data (Apple HealthKit & Google Health Connect): With your explicit permission via the in-app toggle, BetaNatura requests read-only access strictly limited to:
    • Steps (today's step count)
    • Weight / Body Mass (most recent morning weight reading)
    We do not access workouts, heart rate, sleep, nutrition, clinical records, or any other health metrics.
  • Diagnostics & Analytics: Anonymous crash reports and app performance telemetry (via Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics and AppMetrica) to improve app stability.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to display your 30-day capsule journey, hydration, step count, and weight progress chart inside the BetaNatura app. Health data read from Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect is never used for advertising, marketing, or data brokering, and is never sold or shared with third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your account and progress records are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore.

Account & Data Deletion

You have full control over your personal data and can permanently delete your BetaNatura account and all associated logs at any time using either of the methods below:

Method 1: Instant In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the BetaNatura mobile app on your iOS or Android device.
  2. Tap the Account tab in the bottom right navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the screen and tap Delete Account.
  4. Tap Delete Forever in the confirmation dialog. Your Firebase Authentication account, profile, settings, weight logs, water intake, and 30-day capsule history are immediately and permanently erased.

Method 2: Web Account Deletion Request

What Data Is Deleted?

  • Permanently Deleted: Firebase Authentication credentials (email & UID), user profile (`users/{uid}`), reminder settings (`settings/{uid}`), and all daily capsule, water, step, and weight logs (`days/{uid}/log/*`).
  • Revoking Health Permissions: You can also disconnect Apple Health or Health Connect anytime inside the app under Account → Integrations or via your device's system settings.
